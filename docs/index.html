How it works

  1. Join your call

    Escriba turns on Meet’s captions, hides them and gives the space back to the video.

  2. Talk as usual

    Each caption becomes a line with who said it and when. No bot, no audio.

  3. Leave with the notes

    The transcript is saved as Markdown in your Downloads, and on a page to search every meeting.

  4. Ask your AI

    Claude, Cursor or any MCP app reads your meetings and answers with names and times.

Optional

Connect your AI in one line.

Escriba’s MCP server lets Claude, Cursor or any MCP app search and read your meetings, and answer with names and times. 

claude mcp add escriba -- npx -y escriba-mcp

Other apps: setup guide. The server only reads your files; the AI app you connect receives what it reads.

Private by design
